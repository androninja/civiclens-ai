Problem Statement:

Municipal grievance redressal systems suffer from high citizen friction (text-heavy, English-only portals), overwhelming duplicate backlogs for urban local bodies, lack of automated hazard triage, and systemic "ghost ticket resolutions" where cases are closed without verifiable photographic ground proof or citizen dispute oversight.

Brief About the Idea

Closing the municipal accountability loop through zero-friction multimodal intelligence

Overview

how_to_reg Zero-Friction Citizen Experience

  • 30-Second Reporting: Citizens snap a camera photo, record a voice note in Hindi or their mother tongue, and 1-click pin coordinates.
  • Voice Clarity Nudges: Unclear audio (<4 words or muffled) prompts the user with an interactive clarify/override popup.
  • Distress Hotlines: Immediate safety helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416, 1091, 1098) triggered by crisis cues.

domain Municipal Intelligence & Oversight

  • Spatial Deduplication: Haversine (<250m) & Jaccard token clustering merges duplicate reports automatically.
  • Explainable P1–P4 Triage: Gemini AI assigns severity with plain-language technical justifications.
  • Closed-Loop Verification: Field crews submit mandatory "after-repair" photos.
  • Citizen Veto: Citizens retain a 1-click dispute right to reopen unfixed work.

Process Flow & Use-Case Diagram

The 6-stage lifecycle from hazard detection to verified ground closure

Workflow
1. Intake
Multimodal Capture
Citizen snaps photo, records voice memo in native tongue, and 1-click pins GPS coordinates.
2. Gemini AI
Multimodal Triage
Gemini analyzes audio bytes and pixels concurrently: transcribes speech, detects hazards, scores P1-P4.
3. Deduplicate
Spatial Clustering
Haversine (250m) + token similarity clusters repeat complaints without silencing any citizen report.
4. Dispatch
Field Assignment
Triage queue assigns specialized municipal crews (Roads, Sanitation, Water) with strict SLA targets.
5. Verify
Before/After Audit
Crew uploads photo evidence of repair; advisory AI inspects consistency before supervisor sign-off.
6. Citizen Veto
Accountability Loop
Citizen verifies repair on ground. If unfixed, 1-click dispute right reopens case for executive review.

Architecture Diagram of the Proposed Solution

Leveraging Google Cloud Run, Firebase Hosting, Cloud Firestore, and Gemini Multimodal AI

Architecture

1. Client & Edge Tier

  • Angular 19 SPA: Standalone components, reactive signals, Playwright verified.
  • Firebase Hosting: Global edge CDN, SSL, transparent /api/* reverse proxy to Cloud Run.

2. Compute & Security

  • Google Cloud Run: Serverless ASP.NET Core 8 container with auto-scale to zero.
  • ASP.NET Rate Limiting: Token-bucket limiter returns HTTP 429 under flood load.
  • ImageSharp: Enforces 2MB bounds and compresses images to Quality 75 JPEG.

3. AI & Data Tier

  • Gemini Multimodal AI: Processes visual pixels and audio bytes in single calls.
  • Cloud Firestore: Real-time NoSQL complaints and immutable audit logs.
  • Cloud Storage: Encrypted object storage for evidence photos and audio.
