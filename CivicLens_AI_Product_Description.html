1. Brief About the Idea

CivicLens AI is an AI-powered, closed-loop civic intelligence platform engineered to bridge the trust and accountability gap between citizens and municipal authorities.

In communities worldwide, traditional municipal grievance systems fail due to a dual-sided bottleneck:

  • For Citizens: Reporting an issue requires navigating Byzantine multi-page forms, dropdowns with unfamiliar bureaucratic terminology, and rigid English-only interfaces with zero visibility into resolution progress.
  • For Civic Authorities: Field offices are bombarded by thousands of unstructured, vague complaints with high duplication rates (e.g., 20 people reporting the same pothole), obscuring critical emergencies like exposed live wires or collapsed drainage.
  • The Accountability Gap: Tickets are frequently closed with generic "Work Completed" checkmarks without verifiable ground evidence, leading to public distrust.
Core Solution Premise: CivicLens AI provides zero-friction multimodal reporting (live webcam/photo capture and regional voice memos in Hindi and English with automatic GPS positioning). Behind the scenes, Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 parses unstructured input into structured incident parameters. A deterministic Haversine geospatial deduplication engine aggregates neighborhood complaints into single actionable incidents, and mandatory before-and-after photo verification pairs with advisory AI inspection and citizen dispute rights to close the loop.

2. Market & Social Opportunities

Civic infrastructure governance is at an inflection point globally:

  • Urban Local Bodies (ULBs): More than 4,800 municipal corporations in India and 10,000+ municipalities globally manage public infrastructure. Under smart city initiatives (such as India's Smart Cities Mission and Swachh Bharat Urban 2.0), billions in capital expenditure are allocated toward operational automation.
  • Preventing Public Hazards: Rapid identification of open manholes, active electrical sparks, and major water main breaks reduces municipal liability and saves civilian lives.
  • Eliminating Redundant Dispatches: Municipal field crews waste up to 35% of their working hours dispatched to redundant duplicate tickets. Clustering nearby complaints saves fuel, equipment wear, and administrative triage hours.
  • Empowering Marginalized Communities: Blue-collar workers, rural migrants, and non-English speakers gain equal access to governance by speaking directly in their native language (e.g., Hindi) rather than being shut out by bureaucratic portals.

Routed directly to municipal teams (Roads, Water, Lighting, Sanitation) with real-time countdown against P1-P4 SLAs.

6. List of Features Offered by the Solution

6.1 Citizen Portal (Public Experience)

  • Direct Webcam & Device Camera: Live video preview, snapshot capture, retake option, and automated stream release.
  • Multilingual Voice Recorder: Records via browser MediaRecorder, natively supporting Hindi (hi-IN) and English (en-IN) with audio player playback and editable transcript.
  • Interactive GPS Mapping: 1-click HTML5 geolocation ("Use My Location") with interactive pin placement and address auto-fill.
  • Real-Time Case Tracker: Visual status steppers, priority badges, and transparent public audit timelines.
  • Citizen Dispute Redressal: If a ticket was closed prematurely or with shoddy workmanship, citizens click "Request Review" to automatically reopen the case.

6.2 Reviewer & Municipal Dispatch Portal

  • Live Triage Queue: Filter incidents by department, priority, duplicate flag, and real-time SLA countdown timers.
  • Duplicate Linking Engine: Shows match confidence, distance in meters (e.g., "45m away"), and 1-click primary case linking.
  • Department Routing: Instant assignment to specialized civic divisions (Sanitation, Water Supply & Drainage, Roads & Infrastructure, Electrical Safety).
  • Resolution Evidence Upload: Field crews submit repair photos for side-by-side comparison before ticket closure.

6.3 Administration & Governance Dashboard

  • SLA Target Configurator: Adjust required resolution windows (P1: 4h, P2: 12h, P3: 24h, P4: 72h) with instant persistence.
  • System Health Probe: Real-time diagnostics for Gemini AI API, relational database, and storage system.
  • 1-Click Persona Switcher: Instant role switching between Citizen, Reviewer, and Admin for live hackathon judge demonstrations.
  • Immutable Audit Log: Chronological event tracking recording timestamp, actor, role, and rationale for every lifecycle transition.

10. Technologies Used & Technical Rationale

Layer Technology Rationale
Frontend Angular 19 + Angular Material Enterprise-grade reactive signals, strict TypeScript typing, accessible Material design compliant with WCAG 2.1 civic standards.
Backend API ASP.NET Core 8 Web API (C# 12) High-throughput compiled execution, native memory efficiency, and robust dependency injection pipeline.
Generative AI Google Gemini 1.5 / 2.0 Flash Native multimodal support (image + audio + text) with deterministic JSON Schema output enforcement and sub-2s latency.
Database PostgreSQL / SQLite via EF Core 8 SQLite for portable hackathon judging; seamless production migration to PostgreSQL with PostGIS spatial indexing.
Geospatial Maths Haversine Formula (C#) Sub-microsecond mathematical distance calculation over spherical coordinates without requiring heavy external GIS services.
Duplicate NLP Jaccard Token Similarity Fast, explainable word n-gram set intersection over union; resists semantic hallucination and handles typographical variations.
Testing Frameworks xUnit + Moq • Playwright 1.48+ 21 backend unit tests for logic & security; 10 browser end-to-end user journeys testing live webcams and audio.
Cloud Deployment Google Cloud Run & Cloud SQL Serverless container execution that scales to zero when idle, minimizing municipal public-sector computing expenses.
